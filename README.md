# 🚑 BREATHLINE  
### India’s First Verified Emergency Medical Ecosystem

> **“When seconds matter, information saves lives.”**

BREATHLINE is a secure, emergency-first digital healthcare platform designed to provide **instant access to critical medical information** while preserving **patient privacy, data security, and clinical accuracy**.

This project addresses a real-world healthcare problem: **delays in emergency treatment caused by missing or fragmented medical records**.

---

## 🧠 Problem Statement

In medical emergencies, doctors often lack access to:
- Patient allergies  
- Ongoing medications  
- Past surgeries  
- Chronic conditions  

Medical records are scattered across hospitals, paper files, and disconnected systems.  
Existing solutions such as ABHA, DigiLocker, and hospital EMRs fail during real emergencies due to:
- Login requirements  
- No emergency mode  
- No doctor verification  
- Limited interoperability  

This results in **preventable medical errors and delayed treatment**.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview

**BREATHLINE** introduces a **3-second emergency access protocol** combined with:
- Verified doctor authentication  
- Patient-controlled data sharing  
- AI-powered medical insights  
- Secure and auditable access logs  

BREATHLINE is **not a storage app** — it is a **real-time medical ecosystem**.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

### 🚨 Emergency Access
- Encrypted QR-based emergency access  
- No login required  
- Works even if the patient is unconscious  
- Offline-compatible  
- Time-limited & tamper-proof  

### 👤 Patient Control
- Upload & manage medical records  
- Share selective files only  
- Time-based access expiry  
- Revoke access anytime  
- Complete access history  

### 👨‍⚕️ Verified Doctor Ecosystem
- Multi-layer doctor verification  
- Role-based access control  
- Secure doctor dashboard  
- Request–approve workflow  
- Immutable audit trail  

### 🤖 AI-Powered Intelligence
- Prescription OCR (handwritten support)  
- Structured medication history  
- Drug interaction alerts  
- Medical report interpretation  
- Health trend analysis  

### 🔒 Security & Privacy
- End-to-end encryption (AES-256, TLS)  
- JWT & OAuth 2.0 authentication  
- Two-factor authentication  
- Privacy-first, compliance-ready architecture
